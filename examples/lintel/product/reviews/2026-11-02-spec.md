## What works
- builder: the story map covers every backbone step, and the ACs cover many error and permission states with a precise out-of-scope list.
- newcomer: the ACs use Given, When, Then with observable results, including empty, error, no-permission and outage states.
- skeptic: the ACs are concrete and observable, and the pitch's no-gos are kept in out of scope.
- customer: the ACs name clear messages such as "Set an on-call number to get alerts" and "Alert not sent", so failures are not silent.
- ai-risk: the failure paths are stated, and ai-scope.md sets firm limits on what the AI may never do.
