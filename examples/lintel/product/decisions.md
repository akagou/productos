## D-001: Take the emergency visibility bet next, and hold AI reply drafts

- Decision: We will build emergency visibility (detect, pin and alert on likely emergencies) in the next 6-week cycle with 2 engineers and 1 designer, and we will hold AI reply drafts until the revisit date.
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Decided by: Priya Raman, product manager
- Door: Two-way
- Must also agree: Not needed. Sam Ortiz, founder and CEO, is to be told, because the hold changes his request.
- Why: Missed emergencies are the strongest problem in the evidence: 4 of 4 interviews, 6 of 24 tickets and a $2,800 loss. (Observed, E-001; Measured, E-008; Told, E-001 cost) AI replies were not asked for unprompted and a wrong reply cost one firm about $1,900. (Observed, E-002; Told, E-003) The Doorbell PM renewal risk is real but one firm. (Told, E-005) The link from fewer missed emergencies to the North star is a Guess (A-005).
- Options considered:
  1. Emergency visibility, with AI reply drafts held (chosen)
  2. AI reply drafts. Not chosen because evidence is weaker and the accuracy risk is untested (A-006).
  3. Do nothing. Result: urgent messages keep getting lost, and the January renewal risk stays unaddressed.
- Revisit when: 2027-01-15, or earlier if 2 or more firms name AI replies as a reason to leave, or the 450-unit firm's decision names AI replies.
