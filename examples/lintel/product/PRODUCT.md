# Lintel

A shared inbox for tenant email, SMS and web forms, for the office manager at a property management firm.

## Who it is for
The office manager at a property management firm with 50 to 500 units (3 to 15 staff), who answers tenants all day. (Told: team, TEAM.md)

## Their problem
"Tenants reach us everywhere, and the one message that matters gets lost in the noise." (Told: team, TEAM.md)

## What they use today
Gmail, texts on a work phone, a spreadsheet of open repairs, and a whiteboard. (Told: team, TEAM.md)

## How we know it works
Weekly active staff as a share of paid seats: 71% in October 2026. (Measured: product database and Stripe, pulled by Tom, 2026-10-28)
