## Themes
### Missed emergency
- Problem as customers state it: urgent messages sit unseen under routine ones, or never reach the on-call person. (Told)
- "We missed a flood text for 3 hours because it was under 40 rent questions. Can urgent stuff go to the top?" (item T-1001)
- "Lockout text at 11pm went unseen until morning. Tenant was furious." (item T-1014)
- "No heat report from a tenant with a baby, missed for 2 hours. Need alerts." (item T-1019)
- Feature requests: "make emergencies impossible to miss" (T-1024), "Need a way to flag emergencies" (T-1009) -> problem: nothing stops people from overlooking an urgent message; a manual red tag gets forgotten. (Told)
- Opportunity: new, see opportunities.md.

### Repeat questions
- Problem as customers state it: the same routine questions take staff time. (Told)
- "Same parking question 15 times this week. Is there a way to auto answer?" (item T-1003)
- "Repeat questions are 80% of our inbox." (item T-1021)
- Feature requests: auto-answer (T-1003), tenants self-serve trash day (T-1007), pin an answer (T-1012) -> problem: staff retype known answers. (Told)
- Opportunity: new, see opportunities.md.

### Vendor and work orders
- Problem as customers state it: sending a repair to a vendor is slow, and status needs phone calls. (Told)
- "Assigning a repair to a plumber takes 6 clicks and a phone call. Too slow." (item T-1004)
- "Work orders do not show vendor status, we call them to check." (item T-1017)
- "Assigning repairs on mobile is impossible." (item T-1022)
- Opportunity: new, see opportunities.md.

### SMS setup and reliability
- Problem as customers state it: SMS does not connect or sync reliably. (Told)
- "SMS connection failed on setup, gave up." (item T-1013)
- "Could not connect our Twilio number for SMS." (item T-1023)
- "SMS stopped syncing again since Friday." (item T-1005)
- Fits metrics.md: only 48% of new firms connect SMS within 14 days (Measured). SMS is also where urgent tenant texts arrive.
- Opportunity: new, see opportunities.md.

### AI replies
- Problem as customers state it: staff type replies all day. (Told, T-1002)
- "Please add AI replies like Doorbell PM has. My staff spend all day typing." (item T-1002)
- "Would love AI to write replies, but it must not promise repair dates." (item T-1011)
- "AI replies please." (item T-1015)
- Feature request under this theme: AI replies -> problem: reply typing time. T-1011 sets a condition.
- Opportunity: new, see opportunities.md.

### Billing
- "Invoice shows 14 seats, we have 12." (item T-1008)
- "Billing: charge for a removed user." (item T-1020)
- Small fix, not a bet.

## Limits
Vocal customers are over-represented. 24 tickets over 3 months from an unknown number of firms (62 paying). Support tickets over-represent problems with things that break, and under-represent a problem like missed messages the firm never saw. Only one source. No churned customers.
